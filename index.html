Protest Room Sailing Game

Three flash incidents. Hail on time, fly the flag when you must, file before the desk closes, then defend with model boats. Survive the jury.

Tip: procedure fails before rules arguments. Keep your hail loud and your form on time.

How the protest room sailing game works

  1. Hail. An on-water flash card appears. Decide whether to shout “Protest!” right away.
  2. Flag. If your hull is over 6 m, decide whether to fly the red protest flag.
  3. File. Race to the protest desk before the time limit expires.
  4. Jury. Place model boats, pick a one-line defense, and take the verdict: Valid, Invalid, DSQ, Redress, or Penalty already taken.

Two-Turns penalty sailing quiz (quick note)

If the other boat already spun two turns for the same incident, your hearing may end with “penalty already taken.” Causing injury or serious damage is a different story — retiring can be required. This page keeps that as a side beat so the protest-room loop stays short.

Sailing protest procedure FAQ

When must I hail “Protest”?

As soon as reasonably possible after the incident — ideally while boats are still nearby. Silence is how many protests die before the room. See World Sailing RRS Part 5.

When do I need a red flag?

Hull length 6 m or more: display a red flag conspicuously at the first reasonable opportunity. Dinghies often hail-only unless the SIs say otherwise. Check your event’s NoR/SIs.

What makes a protest invalid?

Late hail, missing flag when required, filing after the time limit, muddy incident text, or naming the wrong boat. Procedure can end the case before right-of-way talk starts.

How to protest in sailing racing — the short path?

Hail → (flag if required) → written protest on time → hearing with clear positions. Outcomes include DSQ, redress, invalid protest, or no further penalty after Two-Turns / retirement.

Is this an official World Sailing or RYA quiz?

No. Entertainment / training vibe only. Not a substitute for the Racing Rules of Sailing (RRS 2025–2028) or a real protest committee.
